The verified upstream license is installed here as LICENSE.input-remapper.
The full, unchanged upstream source archive is installed here as
input-remapper-2.2.1-source.tar.gz, with source-lock.json and archive SHA256.
The installed private Python files are the corresponding bridge/extension source.
The native build must also distribute fetch-engine.py --source-archive output,
which includes the upstream archive, complete source tree, bridge, build/staging
code, unit, policy, lock and tests, with a reproducible SHA256.

The upstream module files are unmodified. Runtime-only private extensions disable
stock configuration/daemon entry points and event logging, seed only kernel key
constants, restrict the DSL to trusted generated hold_keys chords and replace
Injector discovery/grab/cleanup. No stock bus, udev autoload, presets or services
are installed, modified, stopped or consulted by the private backend.
